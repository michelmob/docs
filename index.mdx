The ProtosPay Payments API lets your server collect money from customers and send money to beneficiaries. You can:

- **Create checkouts** for an order and see which payment methods the customer can use.
- **Create payments** with a specific payment method, such as Pix, and show the customer the Pix QR code.
- **Send payouts** to a beneficiary's Pix key and follow them until they are paid.

## Environments

| Environment | Base URL | Dashboard |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sandbox | `https://api-sandbox.protospay.com` | [app-sand.protospay.com](https://app-sand.protospay.com) |
| Production | `https://api.protospay.com` | [app.protospay.com](https://app.protospay.com) |

Build and test against the sandbox first. Sandbox and production use separate API keys.
